Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Airports+ ("the App"), developed by Jack Bishop ("we", "us", "our"), handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) NAIPS Credentials

If you choose to use the live weather (NAIPS) feature, you may enter a Requestor ID and password issued by Airservices Australia. These credentials are stored exclusively on your device using your operating system's secure credential store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore / Windows Credential Manager). They are never transmitted to us or any server we operate.

b) Location Data

The App may request access to your device's location to display your position on the map and to evaluate geofence proximity alerts. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

c) User-Created Data

Any entities, fields, notes, or other content you create in the App are stored locally in a database on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.

d) Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google through your app store account. We never receive or store your payment details. Anonymised purchase receipts are validated by our subscription partner, RevenueCat, Inc., solely to confirm that your subscription is active.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • NAIPS credentials are used solely to make authenticated requests to Airservices Australia's NAIPS SOAP API on your behalf when you explicitly request weather data.
  • Location data is used solely to power in-app map and geofence features.
  • User-created data is used solely to display and manage the content you have entered.

3. DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION TO THIRD PARTIES

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties, with the following limited exception:

  • Airservices Australia: When you fetch NAIPS weather data, your Requestor ID and password are transmitted securely (HTTPS) to Airservices Australia's API at www.airservicesaustralia.com. Your use of NAIPS data is also subject to Airservices Australia's own terms and conditions.
  • Apple / Google: Subscription purchases are handled by your device's app store under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: Anonymised receipt and subscription-status data is processed by RevenueCat, Inc. (www.revenuecat.com) to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat never receives your personal details, location, or content.

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

All data collected by the App is stored on your device only. We do not operate any servers or cloud infrastructure that stores your personal information. Sensitive credentials are stored using industry-standard secure storage provided by your operating system.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Data persists on your device until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data and credentials.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This App is intended for licensed pilots and aviation professionals. It is not directed at children under 13 years of age.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected in the App with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
